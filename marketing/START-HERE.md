# Marketing: start here (Dink District)

A plan you can follow in about 20 minutes a day. The goal for the first 30 days
isn't big sales. It's **getting the 8 new designs seen** and **finding out which ones
people like**, so we know whether the designs or something else needs fixing.

---

## Step 1 (today, about 30 min): make the listings convincing
Traffic is wasted if the listing doesn't convince. Do this before any marketing.

1. **Upload the new photos** from `round4-etsy-photos.zip` (one folder per design):
   Etsy → Shop Manager → Listings → open the listing → Photos → add all 4 → drag `1-hero` to the first spot → Save.
   Keep Printify's mockups after ours; they show the real colours.
2. **Shop sections** (Shop Manager → Listings → "+ Add section"):
   - Christmas Sweatshirts
   - Gifts for Grandparents
   - Funny Pickleball Tees
   - Mugs & Stickers

   Put each listing in one section. Buyers browse more when a shop is organised.
3. **Shop basics** (Shop Manager → Settings → Info & appearance):
   - add the logo and banner from `brand/`
   - add the shop announcement and About text from `brand/shop-text.md`

   An empty shop looks untrustworthy.
4. **Turn on Etsy notifications** in the Etsy Seller app, so you answer messages within hours. That matters for ranking and for trust.

## Step 2 (today, about 20 min): Pinterest, our main free traffic source
Pinterest works like a search engine for gift ideas. Pins keep bringing visits for months, and you don't need followers.

1. Go to **business.pinterest.com** → create a free **business account** (name it "Dink District").
2. **Claim your Etsy shop:** Settings → Claimed accounts → Etsy → connect. Your pins then show your shop name and get more trust and reach.
3. Create these **3 boards** (names must match exactly, because the upload uses them):
   - `Pickleball Christmas Gifts`
   - `Pickleball Gifts for Grandparents`
   - `Funny Pickleball Shirts`

   Give each a one-line description with words people search for, e.g. "Funny pickleball Christmas sweatshirts and gift ideas for pickleball players".
4. **Bulk upload the scheduled pins:** Create → Create Pin → **Bulk create Pins** (or "Upload .csv") → choose `marketing/pinterest_round4.csv`.
   - That schedules 24 pins, 2 a day from Oct 7 to Oct 18.
   - Get the file from GitHub (repo → `marketing/pinterest_round4.csv` → Download) or ask me to send it.
   - The older `pinterest_bulk.csv` is for the old designs; skip it.
5. **Every couple of days (5 min):** open Pinterest, search "pickleball gift", and save 3-5 good pins from others to your boards. An active account gets shown more.

When the 24 pins run out (Oct 18), ask me for the next batch (new headlines and the other photos).

## Step 3 (optional, costs money): Etsy Ads for fast answers
Free traffic is slow for a new shop. **Etsy Ads at $1-2 a day for 14 days (about $15-30)** is the fastest way to find out whether people click on the designs, because Etsy shows them to thousands of shoppers.
- Shop Manager → Marketing → Etsy Ads → daily budget $1 → advertise only the 8 new designs.
- Stop it after 14 days if nothing is happening. **Only do this if you're OK spending that.** It's your call.

## Step 4: free extras (pick what you enjoy)
- **Facebook groups:** many pickleball groups ban selling, but some have a "promo day". Read the rules, post only where it's allowed, at most once a week per group. I can write the posts.
- **Instagram or TikTok:** post the listing photos with a short caption, 3-4 times a week. Slow to build, but free. I can write captions and hashtags.
- **Don't** DM strangers, spam groups or post on Reddit's pickleball subreddit (it bans self-promotion). That gets the shop banned.

## Step 5: measure (every Monday, 5 min) and send me screenshots
Etsy → Shop Manager → **Stats**. Set the range to "Last 7 days". Screenshot:
1. the top part (visits, orders, conversion rate)
2. **Traffic sources** (how much comes from Etsy search, Pinterest or ads)
3. **Listings** (scroll down): views and favourites per listing
4. **Search analytics:** the search terms people used to find you

Send them to me with "weekly stats". I'll log them and tell you what to change.

## How we'll know whether the designs need to be better
Every listing goes through: **seen in search → clicked → favourited → bought.** Where it stops tells us what to fix:

| What you see after 2-4 weeks | What it means | What we fix |
|---|---|---|
| Hardly any views (under ~50 per listing) | Not enough people see it yet. That's a traffic problem, not a design problem. | More pins, better titles or tags, maybe Etsy Ads |
| Lots of impressions in search analytics but few clicks (under ~1% click-through) | The first photo or the design doesn't stop the scroll | New first photo or a stronger design |
| Clicks, but almost no favourites (under ~1 per 30 visits) | People look and aren't excited | **The design.** We remake it or drop it. |
| Favourites, but no orders after 150-300 visits | They like it but don't buy | Price, shipping, photos, or no reviews yet |

Rough numbers to keep in mind: most Etsy shops sell to about **1-3 of every 100 visitors**, so one sale takes roughly 50-100 visits. A brand-new shop often gets its first sale somewhere in **weeks 2-6**. Christmas designs pick up from early November.

**Day 14:** first check. Which designs get the most views and favourites?
**Day 30:** decide. Make more of the winners (same style, new jokes), remake or remove the losers, and add the lessons to `knowledge/design-playbook.md`.
