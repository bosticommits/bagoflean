# Marketing: the easy checklist

Tick these off one at a time. Each one is a few clicks.

## Today: part 1, Etsy (about 30 min)

**1. Add the new photos to each new listing**
- Open the zip I sent (`round4-etsy-photos.zip`). It has one folder per design.
- Etsy → **Shop Manager** → **Listings** → click a new listing.
- Scroll to **Photos** → add the 4 photos from that design's folder.
- Drag the photo named **1-hero** to the first spot → **Publish**.
- Repeat for all 8 new listings.

**2. Add your logo and banner**
- Etsy → **Shop Manager** → click the pencil next to **DinkDistrictArt** (bottom left).
- Upload `brand/shop-icon-500.png` as the shop icon and `brand/shop-banner-3360x840.png` as the banner.

**3. Get the Etsy Seller app** on your phone and turn on notifications.

## Today: part 2, Pinterest (about 20 min)

**4. Make a free Pinterest business account**
- Go to **business.pinterest.com** → Sign up → name it **Dink District**.

**5. Connect your Etsy shop (optional)**
- Pinterest → profile picture → **Settings** → **Claimed accounts** → **Etsy** → Claim → log in to Etsy → Allow.
- **Don't use "Claim your website".** That's for your own website and won't work with an Etsy link.
- If Etsy isn't in the list, skip this step. Pins still work without it.

**6. Make 3 boards** (copy these names exactly)
- Pickleball Christmas Gifts
- Pickleball Gifts for Grandparents
- Funny Pickleball Shirts

**7. Upload the pins**
- Pinterest → **Create** → **Create Pin** → **Bulk create Pins** → upload `pinterest_round4.csv` (the file I sent).
- Done. Pinterest posts 2 pins a day for you until Oct 18.

## Every Monday (5 min)

**8. Send me your stats**
- Etsy → **Shop Manager** → **Stats** → set it to **Last 7 days**.
- Screenshot the whole page and send it to me with "weekly stats".
- I'll tell you which designs work and what to change.

## That's it
- Around **Oct 18**, ask me for more pins.
- If you get a message with a link and no yellow **From Etsy** badge, press **Report message**. It's a scam.
- Optional: Etsy Ads ($1 a day) gets you answers faster, but it costs money, so only if you want.
