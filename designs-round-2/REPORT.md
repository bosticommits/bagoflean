**Pattern:** the biggest sellers are (a) personalised, (b) simple text with a relatable "I'd rather be playing" attitude, (c) retro/varsity looks, and (d) embroidered premium items. Cheap print-on-demand tees and sweatshirts routinely show 40-50% "sale" prices ($6.99-$24), so the real price war is at the bottom. Premium/embroidered and personalised items hold $34-$53.

**Christmas is crowded with the obvious.** Listings I saw lean on Santa-hat paddles, tree lights, reindeer antlers, gingerbread, "ugly sweater", plus puns like "Eat, Dink & Be Merry" (466 reviews), "Deck the Halls with Pickleballs" and "Sleigh the Court". Christmas sweatshirts run $7.69-$39.53, with most of the serious ones at $24-$39.

**Saturated jokes (avoid or only use with a new twist):** "I can't, I have pickleball"; soft serve / ice cream; "I have a dinking problem" (shirts, a plush toy, stickers); "Dink responsibly"; "Born to play pickleball, forced to work"; "Where tennis players go to die"; "Never underestimate an old man with a paddle"; "Pickleball is cheaper than therapy"; frog/goose/pickle-with-sunglasses animal memes ("We Dink at Dawn", "Big Dink Energy"); "Playing pickleball improves memory".

**Gaps that looked under-served in what I sampled** (this is observation, not keyword data):
- Doubles-partner miscommunication, forgetting the score or who's serving, and the kitchen as a *Christmas* joke. These are known meme categories in pickleball humour, but the apparel I saw for them was thin or generic.
- Personalised **couples/anniversary** shirts and **family Christmas** matching shirts (strong personalised demand, few polished designs).
- Gift-giver searches: "pickleball grandma/grandpa/nana", "retirement". These have dedicated Etsy result pages, so shoppers search them directly.
- Trending on TikTok Shop: brain-rot meme tees (a "2026" meme pickleball tee appears there) and "Pickleball - not just for retirees" (Gen Z crossover). I did not build to these; they fit a different brand tone.

**Upcoming occasions (from 5 Oct 2026):** Halloween Sat 31 Oct (26 days, too late to rank; skipped); Thanksgiving Thu 26 Nov (52 days); Black Friday Fri 27 Nov / Cyber Monday 30 Nov; Christmas Fri 25 Dec (81 days); New Year Fri 1 Jan; Valentine's Day Sun 14 Feb 2027 (132 days; list early January). Hanukkah begins in early December and I have no design for it (gap; check the exact date before planning). One calendar source advises publishing seasonal items well ahead so Etsy has time to gather views and sales before peak demand, so **list the Christmas designs this week; every day now costs ranking.**

**Garment notes (the only complaint data I found):** the Gildan 18000 crewneck runs true to size with a boxy, relaxed fit, shrinks roughly 1-3% (more with warm wash), and heather colours use a different cotton/poly blend that can affect print adhesion slightly. So: stick to solid colours (I did), say "relaxed unisex fit, order your usual size" in the listing, and give care instructions (cold wash, low heat).

**Competitor price anchors:** tees ~$10-$30, sweatshirts ~$20-$45, embroidered $34-$53, mugs ~$10-$18, stickers ~$2-$5. My suggested prices: tee $27.99, crewneck $42.99, hoodie $49.99, mug $18.99, sticker $4.99, personalised +$2. These sit at the upper-middle of the range and rely on the design quality and personalisation to justify it. Etsy shows many competitors with struck-through "sale" prices; if you want to match that, set a higher list price and run a sale rather than pricing low outright.

### What I checked automatically (all 19)
- Pixel sizes: shirts 4500 x 5400, mugs 2475 x 1155, sticker 3000 x 3000. All RGBA PNG with transparent backgrounds, written at 300 DPI.
- Flat colour only: no gradients, glows or shadows. Every design uses 2-5 distinct inks (the extra off-palette values the checker sees are only anti-aliased edge pixels).
- Thin details: shirts: under 0.5% of inked area is narrower than roughly 20 px (corner tips and star points). Mugs: 2.3-2.9% (small text lines). All small body text was set in a bold weight and thickened.
- Contrast: every main ink against every garment colour listed. Light-garment designs use a deeper coral (#B03A22) and teal (#17695F) so they hold at 3:1 or better on White, Natural, Sand, Light Blue, Light Pink and Ash. Remaining low-contrast items are deliberate: lime balls on light shirts carry a navy outline, red Santa hats and the red DINKER plate carry cream trim, and the gold feathers on the turkey carry a navy outline.
- Etsy rules: every listing has exactly 13 tags, each 20 characters or fewer, letters/numbers/spaces only, no repeats; titles are 84-103 characters including spaces (limit 140).
- Spelling: every word in the artwork was checked twice by hand. No spell-check library was available, so this was a manual pass. The intentional made-up word is DINKMAS.
- Fonts: Noto Sans (Black weight), Poppins Bold/Medium and Lora Bold Italic, all under the SIL Open Font License. Text is rendered into the PNG pixels, so nothing depends on fonts being installed. Note that the Google Fonts site was unreachable from my workspace, so I used the OFL fonts already installed there.

### Things to do before you publish
- **Personalised designs (4):** the PNG contains example text (THE JOHNSONS, SAM & JO, OAKWOOD, JOHNSON / 11). Printify won't swap the text for you; plan on changing the file per order or use a personalisation app. Check which fits your Printify setup.
- Confirm each garment colour name exists for your chosen Printify blank before listing.
- The mockups are simple flat previews I generated, not photographs. Use real Printify mockups for the listing photos.
